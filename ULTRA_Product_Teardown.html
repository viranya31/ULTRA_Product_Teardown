The one-page version

Ultra looks good in the day. At night, nobody answers.

Ultra is an app from Tap Invest where people put money into short-term loans to businesses and other fixed-return products. We studied what it shows, where it helps, and what happens when you have a question out of office hours.

    Our idea, with made-up numbers

    The morning queue, before and after

    Imagine a night of requests. Today they land in one pile for the team at 9:30 am. With the helper, easy ones are answered overnight and the rest are sorted to the right owner, urgent first. ★ Proposed

    Tap a request to see what the helper would do with it.

    The mix of requests is invented to show the idea. Real numbers need Ultra’s own support data. The helper never asks for PAN, Aadhaar or bank details, and never says which deal to pick.

    What to build, in order

    Route first. Then help at night.

    Routing is cheaper, needs no AI, and the helper depends on it. A helper that cannot hand a request to an owner just moves the pile somewhere else. Pass each gate to unlock the next step.

    Who owns what ★ Suggested roles

    The helper’s rules

    Where should each answer come from?

    Three ways to deal with a question. “RAG” is just the first: look up the right help page, then answer from it. Try the quiz, then flip the risk cards.

    Six risks. Tap to flip.

    Background. On 18 Nov 2024 SEBI, India’s market regulator, issued an interim order (made without hearing the other side first) naming four entities, including Purple Petal Invest, which runs Tap Invest. We do not know what happened next. That is why the helper must stay on the support side of the advice line.
